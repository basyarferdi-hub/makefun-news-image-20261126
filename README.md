# Sum up 26 November's world news in one image — make.fun entry

A program that places one image summing up the world news of **Thursday
26 November 2026** at `/output/entry.png` (1024x1024 PNG, ~131 KB).

The image is a one-page news collage drawn for this entry. Its stories, all
reported on 26 Nov 2026: Russia's ballistic-missile strike on Kyiv and the
grain ships hit near Odesa; the fuel-tanker explosion that killed 31 on
Cairo's Ring Road; Israeli strikes killing nine in Gaza; North Korea's Kim
calling for wider tactical nuclear deployment; UK net migration falling to
77,000, a near 30-year low; plus a ticker with the first postponement of
Macy's Thanksgiving parade in 100 years, the FedEx jet that overshot Miami's
runway, the restarted China-Mongolia-Russia rail service, and the Bogor mine
death toll.

- Bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261126
- Run: `python3 main.py` — copies the bundled `entry.png` to
  `/output/entry.png` in the grader, or pass a local path for testing:
  `python3 main.py out/entry.png`
- No network, no dependencies, Python 3.13 compatible. MIT licensed.
